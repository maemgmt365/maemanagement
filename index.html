About Mae Management

“

Mae exists to carry businesses forward, not to dismantle them.

The Mae philosophy

Mae Management LLC is a management and acquisitions firm focused on acquiring, operating, and growing established, service-based businesses with durable fundamentals and long-term relevance.

Mae specializes in construction, construction management, project management, environmental services, and federal-adjacent industries: sectors defined by real operations, skilled workforces, and critical infrastructure. These are businesses built over decades, often by founders whose identities are deeply tied to what they've created.

Mae is not a short-term investment vehicle or a roll-up driven by financial engineering. The firm takes a long-term ownership approach, prioritizing operational continuity, employee stability, and responsible growth, layering in strategic advantages such as federal certifications and Native ownership where they enhance scale without compromising culture.

Each acquisition is evaluated not only on financial performance, but on leadership integrity, workforce strength, and alignment with Mae's stewardship-oriented philosophy. Mae partners with existing teams, promotes from within wherever possible, and remains actively involved at the strategic level.

No. 04

Strategic advantage

Mae is Native-owned. Its founder is an enrolled member of a federally recognized tribe, and Mae holds full ownership of the businesses it acquires, subject only to seller participation where a founder chooses to stay invested.

That ownership is not a footnote. It is the reason Mae targets federal, state, and municipal work. It is also the answer to a question many sellers never think to ask: what happens to your positioning when you sell?

Much of the value in a construction, environmental, or facilities business is tied to relationships with public-sector buyers and the certifications that open those doors. For most acquirers, that positioning ends at closing. For a Native-owned acquirer, it can be re-established and extended, with programs that many buyers cannot access at all.

Several of these programs are grounded in the political status of tribal membership, not race, which is why they were unaffected by the 2025 changes that removed presumptions from the DBE and 8(a) programs. Where an individualized showing is now required, Mae will make it.

Plain language on timing: certifications are earned after closing, and they take time. Some come within weeks, others six to twelve months. We say eligible, not certified, until they're in hand.
Programs Mae companies are positioned to pursue
  • Buy Indian ActSet-aside authority for Indian Economic Enterprises on Bureau of Indian Affairs and Indian Health Service procurement.Requires 51%+ ownership by members of a federally recognized tribe
  • Indian Incentive ProgramA 5% incentive paid to Department of Defense prime contractors for subcontracts awarded to Indian-owned enterprises. That makes a Mae company a preferred subcontractor, not a charitable one.DFARS 226.1
  • SBA 8(a) Business DevelopmentSole-source and set-aside access for socially and economically disadvantaged owners, via individualized narrative under current rules.
  • HUBZoneWhere an acquired company's location and workforce qualify.
  • DBE & MBETransportation DBE and private-sector MBE (NMSDC) where the business's work warrants it, under the individualized standards now in effect.
